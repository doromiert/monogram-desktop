# User-forward attribution

Desktop direct-text and poll forwarding follows mobile's
[compatibility profile 1](../../mobile/docs/protocols/FORWARDING.md), with the
same parser, sender-label limits and signed-32-bit timestamp range. This is the
forwarder's claim; it does not authenticate the original sender.

## Sending and presentation

The message menu opens a native conversation chooser with a **Hide Sender**
switch. A forward gets a new message ID and the destination's encryption policy;
it leaves the ordinary composer/reply draft intact. Plaintext and explicit OMEMO 2
direct chats are supported. Protected sends never fall back to plaintext.

Outgoing attribution uses the existing mobile-compatible body convention:

```text
↪ Forwarded from alice@example.org · 2023-11-14T22:13:20Z

Original content
```

The prefix is removed for display. Its header opens sender/date details with a
full-address copy control. Literal payload whitespace is preserved. Full known
entity addresses are retained rather than local roster aliases; direct original
senders use their bare address. Room-occupant identity handling is preserved by
the attribution helper, but live desktop room transport is not implemented.
Re-forwarding retains the existing original claim, including an unknown date
(zero). It does not infer a missing address from the latest forwarder. Hide Sender
omits the original claim and sends a fresh copy.

Forwarded polls are fresh open structured definitions, without old ballots or
closure. Historical canonical body polls are recognized after the attribution
prefix is removed; copying/closing them retains their historical representation.
Malformed/noncanonical headers remain literal text. The earlier desktop
prototype's date-less prefix cannot prove attribution and remains literal text.

## XMPP and trust boundaries

[XEP-0297](https://xmpp.org/extensions/xep-0297.html) supplies the existing standard
for receiving standalone structured quotes. Desktop advertises its namespace and
renders one quoted message with an optional outer annotation and original delay.
Quoted IDs, actions, receipts, presence and device hints are never processed as an
original stanza. The actual outer author, ID, date, replies and encryption key
retain their authority. Nested forwarding and non-message content are not
interpreted. Trusted carbon/MAM unwrapping continues through its separate path.

Authenticated OMEMO 2 content may carry the standalone forwarded element. The
same explicit allowlist entry now exists in mobile. Unprotected outer forwarding
XML cannot introduce a claim into decrypted content. Outbound structured
selection/discovery remains absent in both clients; outgoing forwards use the
readable compatibility body inside the selected encryption envelope.

Attribution is persisted with the message, survives reconciliation/reload and
follows authenticated corrections. Retraction scrubs it along with payloads.
No database schema replacement or account-history rewrite is required.

## Verification and open work

Forty-two vectors generated by the actual mobile parser/model check canonical and
malformed headers, Unicode/UTF-16 limits, whitespace, empty payloads, translated
bodies, standalone annotations and inner-authority isolation. Eight also compare
mobile-generated protected SCE payloads. The generator parses serialized XML
before deriving expectations, including XML's line-ending normalization.

The private Prosody/GTK fixture exercises the actual Forward context action,
conversation chooser, Hide Sender, sender details/copy, fresh wire IDs, durable
claims/unknown dates, preserved draft/payload whitespace and blocked attachment
key reuse in plaintext and OMEMO 2. Unit storage checks cover correction and
retraction metadata. Fixture identities and clipboard are private and disposable.

Live media/file forwarding remains unavailable until fresh download/verification
and upload exist; SFS/OOB and bare `aesgcm` references are refused rather than
reusing original key-bearing URLs. Rich entities, captions, albums, room transport,
outbox uncertainty, and physical mobile/desktop UI exchange remain open. This
milestone does not establish complete forwarding or client parity.
